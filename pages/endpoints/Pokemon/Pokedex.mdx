<Callout type="info">Provide either `id` or `pokemon`. If both are given, `id` is used.</Callout>

The pokedex covers every Pokémon from Generation 1 to 9, plus alternate forms (Mega, regional, Gigantamax and others). Forms share their species' `id`, so `?id=` always returns the base species. Use `?pokemon=` to get a form, e.g. `?pokemon=venusaur-mega`.

<Callout type="warning">
A few alternate forms are missing some fields, which are `null` when they are:

- Regional forms (Alolan, Galarian, Hisuian, Paldean) have no `species` or `description`, since those differ from the original form.
- Some newer Mega Evolutions have no `base_experience`.
- A handful of forms have no sprite, and some have no `animated` sprite.

</Callout>
